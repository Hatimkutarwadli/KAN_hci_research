# P1-6 — Statistical analysis of Stage 9 missing-data robustness (SYNTHETIC data; EXTENSION)

All data are simulated. No clinical or causal claim. Original Stage 8/9 experiments, checkpoints, data and reports are untouched (hash/mtime manifest test).

## Method
- **Predictions** regenerated by forward-only inference from the existing checkpoints (no training): original GRU + finalists R0O0T1, R0O2T1, R0O3T1; 5 seeds; main and forecast parts; test split; clean control C0 + the 10 panel scenarios; origins 27..119 stride 2; mask replicates as in Stage 9. Stored in `predictions/`.
- **Reproduction gate (run first):** all 30,600 metric values recomputed from the regenerated predictions match the saved Stage 8/9 CSVs, max |diff| = 1.8e-15 (tolerance 1e-5). Panel-mean cells also match `final_test_report.md` to its 2-decimal rounding (max dev 0.0050 pct-points).
- **Participant uncertainty:** participant-cluster bootstrap, B = 2,000, one shared participant-count matrix for every model, seed, scenario, replicate and task (fully paired). Intervals are *conditional on the five trained seeds*.
- **Seed variability (separate):** per-seed estimate SD, range and number of seeds better. Supplementary two-level bootstrap (participants + seeds) is shown only for orientation; with 5 seeds it is unstable.
- **Confirmatory family:** 25 contrasts = 5 families (severity, status, onset, forecast severity, forecast status) x 5 contrasts (R0O0T1/R0O2T1/R0O3T1 vs GRU; R0O2T1 and R0O3T1 vs R0O0T1), panel mean over 10 scenarios. p = two-sided centred paired bootstrap test, p = (1+#{|T_b-T_0|>=|T_0|})/(B+1) (floor about 0.0005); **Holm** over 25; Bonferroni-simultaneous intervals reported.
- **Exploratory:** 275 per-scenario cells (unadjusted intervals; BH q = 0.10); risk-inflation (50 cells, BH).
- **Secondary descriptive (not confirmatory, unadjusted):** clean-control non-inferiority vs the original GRU, margin = Stage 9's rule (GRU seed SD % of mean on clean calibration metrics).
- **Participant consistency (descriptive, no p-values):** share of participants improved, median loss difference, top-10 % concentration, and subgroups = thirds of mean TRUE nowcast severity (labels only; fixed before any model comparison).

## Findings
**Confirmatory (Holm 0.05): 7 of 25 survive; all are severity RMSE.**
- Nowcast severity: R0O0T1, R0O2T1, R0O3T1 each better than the GRU (-1.40 %, -1.66 %, -1.52 %; participant 95 % intervals exclude 0; 5/5 seeds better). R0O2T1 vs R0O0T1 -0.27 % [-0.39, -0.15], 5/5 seeds. R0O3T1 vs R0O0T1: not distinguishable from 0.
- Forecast severity: R0O0T1 (-0.75 %) and R0O2T1 (-1.05 %) vs GRU, and R0O2T1 vs R0O0T1 (-0.31 %), Holm-significant. R0O3T1 vs GRU (-0.85 %) is significant only unadjusted (Holm p = 0.54; Bonferroni interval includes 0).
- **Status AP, onset AP/AUROC and forecast status: no confirmatory contrast is distinguishable from 0.** Intervals are wide (roughly +-1.5 to 3 %), seed-to-seed SD is as large as the effects, and several point estimates favour the *original GRU* (onset: R0O3T1 is worse than the GRU in 5/5 seeds, +2.5 %, unadjusted p = 0.036, Holm p = 0.60). The Stage 9 "improvement" claims for status and onset are therefore **not supported** by this analysis; they are consistent with no difference.
- Variance: for severity, participant sampling variance exceeds between-seed variance of the seed mean; for status/onset against the GRU the two are comparable.

**Exploratory scenarios (50 of 275 cells flagged at BH q = 0.10; about 5 expected false):** nowcast severity 22 flagged (21 improvements, 1 worse) and forecast severity 19 (18 improvements, 1 worse), concentrated in the long/block outages (S2_r40_L7, S3_k7, S3_k14, S5_blk40), S1_r40 and S4_cardiac_absent. S4_activity_absent and C0 show no improvement (point estimates slightly worse). Status: 2 flagged (both worse than the reference); onset: 7 flagged (all worse); forecast status: 0.

**Secondary clean non-inferiority (descriptive):** the margin is met by the Stage 9 point rule in 5 of 7 cases but the one-sided upper 95 % participant bound is within the margin in only 2 (onset AP R0O0T1; forecast-severity R0O2T1). Status finalists exceed the margin on the clean data (+2.7 %, +2.3 %); nowcast status R0O3T1 is worse than the GRU in 5/5 seeds. Non-inferiority is **not established** for most families by this analysis.

**Risk inflation** (mean predicted onset probability / own clean control; panel-mean ratio difference vs the GRU, negative = less inflation): R0O0T1 -0.036 [-0.043, -0.031] (5/5 seeds), R0O2T1 -0.017 [-0.027, -0.008] (4/5), R0O3T1 -0.009 [-0.032, +0.012] (5/5; interval includes 0). R0O2T1 and R0O3T1 inflate MORE than R0O0T1 (+0.019 and +0.027, 0/5 seeds less). By scenario the direction is not uniform: R0O2T1/R0O3T1 inflate more than R0O0T1 in S3_k7, S3_k14, S5_blk40 and S2_r40_L7 (`analysis/risk_inflation.md`). Descriptive caveat metric only; no calibration claim. (Correction 2026-11-16: an earlier version of this paragraph said every finalist inflates less than the GRU on the panel mean; that was wrong for R0O3T1.)

**Participant consistency (descriptive):** improvements are not uniform. For nowcast severity about 60-65 % of participants improve (panel mean: 0.62, 0.65, 0.60 for R0O0T1, R0O2T1, R0O3T1 vs the GRU); the gain comes almost entirely from the lowest-severity third (0.94-0.96 improved) while the highest-severity third improves in only about 0.38 (the original GRU is better for many high-severity participants). Top-10 % shares are large (0.6-0.8) for finalist-vs-GRU; where the net total improvement is near zero the share is undefined or above 1 (uninterpretable, shown as printed). Status contrasts show the same low-vs-high severity gradient.

## Limitations
- Test split was already used for Stage 9 reporting; this is a re-analysis, not independent confirmation, and is conditional on the finalists chosen on calibration.
- Only 5 seeds: seed-level inference is weak (sign-test floor p = 0.0625); participant-level intervals are conditional on those seeds.
- 200 test participants; 31-32 with sustained-onset events (`analysis/event_counts.md`); status AP/onset AP are noisy. No degenerate resamples occurred.
- Centred bootstrap p-values have a floor about 0.0005; Holm assumes the 25 contrasts are the complete confirmatory family.
- Panel-mean estimand weights the 10 scenarios equally; per-scenario results are exploratory.
- Synthetic data only; no generalisation to real wearable data and no clinical claim.
